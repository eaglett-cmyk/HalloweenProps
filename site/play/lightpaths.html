Cave Lightpaths

Lead a glow from the Hoard Beacon to a door between worlds. Every step costs energy. Light lanterns on the way, or save energy for points. Can you find the best path?

Energy
12
Points0

Tap a square next to the beacon to start the path.

How the points work
  • A plain step costs 1 energy. Candy rubble costs 2. Big rocks block the way.
  • Each lantern you light: +2.
  • The near door: +2. The far door: +4. Reaching a door ends the path.
  • Every bit of energy you still have at the door: +1.
  • You can't step on the same square twice. The best path is not always the longest!

Today's cave is the same for everyone, so you can compare paths with your family. Your first delivery each day sends a little light to the Hoard Beacon; better paths win ribbons.
